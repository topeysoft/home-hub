The material

Nightfall glass

One pane, six layers, always in this order. The blur is what the pane is made of; the rim is its edge catching the light; the sweep is that light travelling across it; the inner shade is its thickness; the drop is the air underneath. Take any one away and the rest stops reading as glass.
