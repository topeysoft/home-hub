How the house tells them apart, and why that is not a guess

Every Matter device announces a vendor and a product id, so the house can recognize our strip whoever set it up — it does not have to infer anything from behavior. What it cannot see from that alone is whether it can talk to one, so the honest test is the one that matters: has this strip ever spoken to our broker? One that has is the left column. One that has not is the right, and it may move left later if somebody sets it up here again.
