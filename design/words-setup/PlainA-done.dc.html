Temi’s house is ready.

Leave this screen on the wall. It dims to a clock after a few minutes; touch it to wake it.

Point the phone’s camera at the code, or open http://hub.local.

On iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
On Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.

The house becomes an app on the phone, with its own icon. The phone needs to be on your home Wi‑Fi.
  • Type what you want into Tell the house… at the top of the screen: “Living room lights off”, “is the front door locked?”.
  • Plug in something new and a line at the top of the screen says so.
  • Devices that don’t know their room wait in New devices until you place them.
